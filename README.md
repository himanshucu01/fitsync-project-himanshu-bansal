# FitSync - Health Analytics Platform 🚀

### Transforming Personal Health Data into Real-time Insights

## Project Overview
FitSync is an intuitive personal health analytics dashboard designed to provide individuals with actionable insights into their health data. Built with Python and Streamlit, FitSync offers a seamless experience through its three pages: Main, Dashboard, and Trends. Users can explore dynamic KPIs and charts on the Dashboard page, while the Trends page offers in-depth histograms and insights into their health metrics. FitSync is the perfect tool for those looking to understand their health patterns and make informed decisions based on real-time analytics.
